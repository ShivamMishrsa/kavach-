# Pitch deck notes

The deck is a private slide deck made in Claude (not stored in this repository):
<https://claude.ai/artifact/TedmLw4UJDdZyjDd51PDQu>. Only the owner can open it until it is shared from
its Share menu. This page keeps what the deck says, and the rules it follows, in the repository.

**Rules for any pitch about Kavach** (same as `docs/resume-bullets.md`):

- Show the limits slide, and do not hurry it. A project that lists its own weaknesses is believed.
- Every number has a source (`docs/benchmarks.md`) and its caveat on the slide: "messages the team wrote".
- No accuracy percentage for real messages, no "users", no market size, no loss statistics unless you
  have checked them yourself (verify).
- The business slide is a **concept** and says so.

## The 12 slides

| # | Slide | The point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Kavach, check before you pay; working, not live |
| 2 | The problem | Badge, video call, deadline: the "digital arrest" script. No invented statistics |
| 3 | Two phones | One phone has the caller, the other needs an answer without hanging up: 4 steps |
| 4 | What it does | Check, explain, report; no AI model decides; 7 languages |
| 5 | Built for a bad connection | Installable, opens offline, keeps a check for later, saves data |
| 6 | Privacy by design | Consent first, 15 minutes in memory, guest mode, one-button delete |
| 7 | How it works | Phone, API, reading, judging; the reasons shown are the reasons used |
| 8 | Measured, honestly | 73% (19 of 26) on a fresh set, 7 of 26 "low risk"; the 100% was wrong about generalising |
| 9 | Also measured | 41% to 7% wrong characters; 1.5 s to 0.7 s; 3 s to milliseconds; 0 axe violations |
| 10 | What it cannot do | Rules not a model, false alarms, not live, drafts, no real phone |
| 11 | A concept for businesses | Mock: the engine as an API for banks and telecoms; not built |
| 12 | What would make it real | Go live, check with people, measure on real data; try the 2-minute demo |

## Shorter versions

- **3 minutes:** slides 1, 2, 3, 8, 10, 12. Say the 73% out loud.
- **30 seconds:** "People get called by a fake officer who says you are under digital arrest and must pay.
  Kavach lets you check the notice on a second phone, in your language, and tells you the risk and
  why, never 'this is a scam'. It works offline, deletes everything after 15 minutes, and I measured
  it honestly: on messages I had not seen it caught 73% of the scams it was built for, so I fixed the
  gaps and wrote down what is still missed."

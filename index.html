1. Executive Summary & Problem Breakdown (Asli Problem Kya Hai?)

India me cyber fraud ab basic lottery SMS ya fake OTP calls tak seemit nahi raha. Scammers high-scale, psychologically weaponized aur automated techniques use kar rahe hain jo seedha aam nagrikon aur business operations ko destabilize karti hain.

1.1 Major Threat Vectors (Scammers Ka Tareeka)

  • "Digital Arrest" Extortion Syndicates: Organised cartels victims ko video call (Skype/WhatsApp) par connect karke fake CBI, ED, State Police ya Supreme Court ke rooms create karte hain. Wo victim ko fake arrest warrants, official letterheads aur fabricated court stamps dikhate hain aur ghanton tak call par bandhe rakhte hain jab tak victim apni sari life savings "verification account" me transfer na kar de.
  • Neural Voice Cloning & Impersonation: Scammers social media reels ya shorts se 5–10 seconds ka audio sample utha kar Generative AI (Zero-Shot TTS) se voice clone kar lete hain. Fir parents ya doston ko call karke bolte hain: "Bhaiya ka accident ho gaya hai, turant 50,000 bhejo." Normal call filters ise normal call samajhkar pass kar dete hain.
  • Indic Language & Dialect Masking: Scammers pure English me baat nahi karte. Wo pure North India me Bhojpuri, Maithili, Haryanvi aur pure Hinglish slang use karte hain. Global cyber protection engines (jaise Norton, McAfee, Cloudflare) Indic multi-lingual semantics samajh nahi pate.
  • "Golden Hour" Panic & Evidence Collapse: Jab fraud execute hota hai, pehle 60–120 minute sabse crucial hote hain (Golden Hour). Agar is time me UTR number, beneficiary UPI ID aur transaction timestamp I4C helpline (1930) par submit kar diya jaye, toh bank funds freeze ho jate hain. Par aam insan panic me aa kar screen capture aur bank SMS collect nahi kar pata, aur paise multi-layer mule accounts me siphon off ho jate hain.

1.2 Solution Mechanism (Hum Kya Bana Rahe Hain?)

Project Kavach ek 100% Cloud-Native Indic Multi-Modal Software Engine hai jisme do direct entry points hain:

  1. Kavach Citizen Security Web / PWA: Ek fast, zero-install Progressive Web App jahan citizen koi bhi recording, fake notice ki photo, phishing link ya screenshot forward/upload karega. System turant plain Hindi/Hinglish me scam expose karega aur ek standardized Pre-FIR Evidence Dossier PDF bana dega jo direct 1930 ya cybercrime.gov.in me upload ho sake.
  2. Kavach Enterprise B2B API Engine: Sub-200ms latency wale microservices jo commercial banks, UPI apps (PhonePe, GPay), NBFCs aur telcos ko license kiye jayenge, taaki onboarding (eKYC) aur high-risk fund transfers ke time deepfake voice aur tampered documents real-time block ho sakein.

1.3 Why Hardware Was Scrapped? (Software-Only Decision)

  • TLS 1.3 Encryption Barrier: Network cables par sara traffic end-to-end encrypted hota hai. Ek hardware firewall (jaise Raspberry Pi) bina har phone me private root certificate install kiye WhatsApp audio ya bank receipts inspect hi nahi kar sakta. Software directly application layer par unencrypted file ko analyze karta hai.
  • Zero Manufacturing Deficit: Hardware me physical shipment, chip inventory, return logistics aur hardware warranty ka jhanjhat hota hai. Pure software model 85%+ gross margin deliver karta hai.
  • Enterprise Budget Alignment: Chote kirana dukandar hardware box kharidne ke liye paise nahi dete. Lekin commercial banks fraud rokhne ke liye high-value B2B API contracts sign karte hain.

2.1 Multi-Modal AI Detection Subsystems

  • Indic Speech Pipeline: AI4Bharat IndicWhisper use karta hai jo raw audio ko text me convert karta hai. Ye model English-Hindi code-mixing (Hinglish) aur regional colloquial terms ko accurately recognize karta hai. Saath hi AASIST (Audio Anti-Spoofing using Integrated Spectro-Temporal Graph Attention Networks) model waveform ke linear prediction cepstral coefficients aur high-frequency phase shifts ko map karke synthetic voice detect karta hai.
  • Document Forensics Engine: PaddleOCR document ke Devanagari aur English text ko extract karta hai. Ek fine-tuned Vision Transformer (ViT) notice par bane State Emblems, police badges aur stamps ko check karta hai ki wo authentic government designs se match karte hain ya internet se cut-paste kiye gaye hain.
  • Statutory Consistency Regex Engine: Notice me mention kiye gaye laws ko match karta hai. Scammers aksar purane Indian Penal Code (IPC) ke invalid sections ya fabricated sections likh dete hain. Hamara parser legal discrepancies pakad kar turant alert karta hai.
  • Phishing & Link Deconstructor: Suspicious links ko parse karke unka age lookup, SSL certificate issuer aur Punycode homoglyphs (jaise SBI ke badle sbı.com) analyze karta hai.

6. Financial Architecture & Unit Economics (Paisa Kaise Banega?)

6.1 Why PWA Trumps WhatsApp Business API

WhatsApp Business API har interaction par platform charges lagata hai. India me 1,000 monthly utility messages ke baad har user interaction par Meta charges lagte hain. Agar 10 lakh users Kavach bot par 3–4 messages chat karenge, toh har query par messaging cost plus GPU compute cost mila kar ₹1.50–₹2.50 kharch honge. Mahine ka operational deficit ₹15 lakh se ₹25 lakh ho jayega bina kisi direct revenue ke.

PWA Strategy: Kavach Progressive Web App use karega. Cloudflare CDN ke through web traffic delivery cost sirf ₹0.02 per user aati hai. WhatsApp ko sirf marketing links share karne ke liye use kiya jayega, jisse distribution expense 95% kam ho jata hai.

6.2 Revenue Generation: B2B Enterprise API

  • Commercial Banks & NBFCs: eKYC verification aur video call onboarding ke waqt deepfake face aur cloned voice verify karne ke liye per-call API rate (₹1.50 se ₹3.00) charge kiya jata hai.
  • UPI & Payment Apps (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm): Payment transfer screen par recipient handle aur user-uploaded payment screenshot verification API integrate hoti hai.
  • Telecom Security Feeds: Telecom companies ko real-time Indic scam scripts aur newly emerged malicious domain feeds license kiye jate hain.

7. Regulatory, DPDP Act 2023 & Legal Shielding

7.1 DPDP Act 2023 Mandatory Compliance

  • Data Fiduciary Classification: Jab user apni call recording ya document upload karta hai, Kavach legal terms me Data Fiduciary banta hai. Platform Section 6 ke mutabiq multi-lingual explicit consent screen enforce karta hai.
  • Biometric Data & Purpose Limitation: Audio me vocal prints biometric data hote hain, aur notice me PAN ya account details hoti hain. Kavach strict Purpose Limitation implement karta hai: analysis score generate hote hi user payload memory se cryptographically shred kar diya jata hai.
  • Penalty Shielding: DPDP Act ke Schedule 1 ke tahat data breach par ₹250 crore tak ki statutory penalty ho sakti hai. Kavach zero persistent customer storage model par chalta hai aur local Indian data centers me operate karta hai.

7.2 Asymmetric Liability Shield (Court/Police Disclaimers)

  • False Negative Risk: Agar system kisi naye advanced scam ko clean bol de aur user paise transfer kar de, toh platform par financial loss ka claim na ho sake.
  • False Positive Risk: Agar system kisi asli court summon ya tax notice ko fake bol de aur citizen court me pesh na ho, toh platform par legal obstruction ka case na bane.
  • Mitigation Strategy: Output hamesha probabilistic risk assessment ke roop me diya jata hai: "Risk Rating: 94%. Is document me unauthorized stamps paye gaye hain. Kripya kisi bhi payment se pehle official 1930 helpline par verify karein." Platform absolute verdict claim nahi karta.
